# Roshni knowledge base (curated, as of 2026-11-11)

## Glossary
- **W / kW**: Power at a moment. 1 kW = 1,000 W.
- **kWh / unit**: Energy over time. One "unit" on a Pakistani bill = 1 kWh.
- **kWp**: Rated peak capacity of a solar array under standard test conditions.
- **On-grid inverter**: Feeds solar to home and grid; normally shuts down in an outage (load-shedding), so it gives NO backup.
- **Hybrid inverter**: Coordinates solar, grid and battery; can supply backup during load-shedding.
- **Off-grid inverter**: Works without the grid, with batteries; cannot export.
- **MPPT**: Inverter input that tracks the panels' best operating point; each has voltage and current limits.
- **Voc / Vmp / Isc**: Panel open-circuit voltage, maximum-power voltage, and short-circuit current. Voc rises on cold mornings, so too many panels in one string can exceed the inverter's maximum voltage.
- **LV / HV battery**: Low-voltage (typically 48 V) or high-voltage battery systems; an inverter supports one or the other.
- **Usable kWh**: Battery energy actually available after depth-of-discharge limits. Always compare batteries on usable kWh.
- **Self-consumption**: Solar energy used directly at home. Under net billing this is worth about four times an exported unit.
- **Net metering (legacy)**: Pre-9 Feb 2026 arrangement where exported units offset imported units 1:1 within agreement terms. Existing agreements keep their terms until expiry.
- **Net billing**: Post-9 Feb 2026 arrangement for new users: imports charged at retail, exports credited at a lower buyback rate (~Rs 10 to 11 per unit in 2026, verify).
- **Buyback rate**: Price paid for each exported unit under net billing.
- **Protected consumer**: Domestic consumer at or below 200 units/month, with lower rates; crossing 200 units once can cost protected status for six months. Government has signalled it may abolish the category by 2027.
- **Fixed charge**: Monthly charge owed regardless of units used (Rs 200 to 675 depending on category/slab, from Feb 2026). Solar cannot remove it.
- **FPA / QTA**: Fuel price adjustment / quarterly tariff adjustment added to bills.
- **Sanctioned load**: Maximum load approved for the connection; affects phase and approvals.
- **DISCO / K-Electric**: Distribution companies (e.g. LESCO, IESCO, MEPCO); K-Electric serves Karachi.
- **A-grade / B-grade**: Market terms for panel quality bins; B-grade panels may have defects or lower output.
- **AEDB**: Alternative Energy Development Board; publishes certified installer lists.
- **Balance of system**: Structures, cables, protection, earthing and other non-major items.

## Bijli gayi to kya hoga? (What happens in load-shedding?)
- On-grid system: during load-shedding the inverter switches off for safety, even in bright sun. No backup.
- Hybrid system with battery: keeps chosen appliances running from battery and solar.
- Off-grid system: runs on solar and battery only; no grid export.
- A battery is sized by the appliances you need during outages and for how many hours, not by your total monthly units.

## Net billing basics
- If you sign a new agreement after 9 Feb 2026 you are on net billing. Exported units earn far less than imported units cost.
- So: use solar during the day (run washing machine, water motor, iron, AC in daytime), avoid oversizing just to export, and consider a battery to move daytime solar to the evening.
- Many installers still quote savings using old 1:1 net-metering logic. Ask them to recalculate for net billing.

## Questions every buyer should ask an installer
- Exact brand and model number of panels, inverter and battery, with datasheets.
- How many panels per string and is the cold-morning voltage within the inverter limit?
- Which DC and AC breakers, surge protectors and cables (size/brand) are included?
- How many earthing pits?
- What structure gauge/coating, and how is it anchored?
- Who honours each warranty in Pakistan, in writing?
- Are the panels A-grade from an authorised distributor? Can I verify serial numbers on the maker's website (e.g. LONGi "One Module One Code")?
- Are net-billing application, meter and DISCO fees included?
- Can payment be split by delivery and commissioning?

## Red flags
- Pressure to sign today; vague one-line quotes; no model numbers; savings that assume 1:1 net metering for a new connection; unwritten warranties; very large advance payments.

## Safety (always hand off)
- Burning smell, sparking, water ingress, hot cables, structural cracks: switch off and call a qualified electrician / PEC-registered engineer. Roshni cannot diagnose these remotely.
